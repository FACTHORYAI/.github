## FACTHORY

**Build the brain behind every operation.**

FACTHORY is the operational intelligence platform for enterprise AI. It connects your
systems, documents, media and expert knowledge into a living model of your operations.
AI agents use that model to research, answer operational questions, generate SOPs and
support decisions, and people stay in charge of what they do. FACTHORY runs as a managed
cloud service, in your own cloud, or fully on premises. [facthory.com](https://facthory.com)

### Public repositories

- [guides](https://github.com/FACTHORYAI/guides): official how-to guides for getting
  started with multiplayer agents, knowledge capture and operational intelligence.
- [muster](https://github.com/FACTHORYAI/muster): multiplayer AI agent rooms for
  Microsoft Teams, with scoped memory and shared permissions, plug-and-play on Mastra.
- [voelundr](https://github.com/FACTHORYAI/voelundr): a fork of Grok Build, the terminal
  AI coding agent, improved for durable workflows.
- [engineering-principles](https://github.com/FACTHORYAI/engineering-principles): our
  guidelines for building large-scale, data-heavy applications.

FACTHORY is built by [Bandpey](https://github.com/bandpey-hq).
